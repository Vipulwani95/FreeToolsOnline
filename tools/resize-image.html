How to use

  1. Upload your image by clicking the upload area or dragging a file.
  2. Select your preferred resizing method (Dimensions or Percentage).
  3. If using Dimensions, enter the new width or height. Keep "Lock Aspect Ratio" checked to avoid distortion.
  4. If using Percentage, adjust the slider to scale the image up or down.
  5. Check the live preview and the new dimensions below it.
  6. Click "Download Resized Image" to save your result.
